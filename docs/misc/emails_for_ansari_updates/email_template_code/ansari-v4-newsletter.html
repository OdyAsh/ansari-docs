Ansari V4: Building the Future of Islamic AI Learning

We're excited to share that development of Ansari V4 is underway! Based on your feedback and the evolving AI landscape, we're focused on creating a more personalized, engaging experience that keeps users returning for deeper Islamic learning.

While V3 established Ansari as a trusted Islamic AI assistant, V4 will transform how you interact with Islamic knowledge through advanced features that adapt to your learning style and needs.

Major User-Facing Improvements

Here are the key improvements you'll experience in Ansari V4:

Smart Learning Guidance

Personalized responses based on your madhab preferences, learning style, and previous conversations

File Upload & Analysis

Upload PDFs, images, and Islamic texts for discussion and scholarly analysis with proper citations

Lightning-Fast Responses

Progressive response mode delivers quick initial answers, then enhances with citations and detailed analysis

Contemporary Issues Support

Curated web search for modern fatawa and current Islamic discussions from trusted sources

Multiple AI Models

Choose between different AI models for optimal cost, speed, and response quality

Interactive Learning Modes

Guided discovery mode that helps you find answers through Socratic questioning rather than direct answers

Behind the Development

V4 development focuses on solving user retention while leveraging cutting-edge AI capabilities. We're moving beyond simple Q&A to create an engaging, personalized learning companion.

Key technical improvements include migration to modern frameworks, enhanced conversation memory, intelligent content compression for longer sessions, and integration with emerging standards like Model Context Protocol (MCP) for seamless workflow integration.
